# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-25)

First release.

- Wolfenstein 3D (Wolf4SDL), native on Apple silicon Macs, drawn with Metal at
  the display's full resolution and frame rate (60 fps on most Macs, 120 fps
  on ProMotion displays)
- The GT/Activision, Apogee and shareware editions recognised automatically
- Adaptive resolution that keeps the frame rate under load
- A compact, pixel-perfect status bar card
- Gore: blood, stains, fragments, hit reactions and its own sounds, spread
  over every CPU core
- Game controllers: DualSense haptics and adaptive triggers, Xbox, DualShock
  and others; the controller used last plays
